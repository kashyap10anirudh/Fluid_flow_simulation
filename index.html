View

Model: 2-D incompressible flow averaged across the channel gap, with Hele-Shaw wall friction (12μ/h²). The plate is flat, without chevron corrugations. Inflow is spread evenly over the inlet port area and leaves through the outlet port. Grid: 2 mm cells.
